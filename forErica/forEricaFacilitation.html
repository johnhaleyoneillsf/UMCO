Facilitation Skills


Expressive Arts as a Tool for Social Change




Exercises in creative self-expression and guidance to design a community workshop in expressive arts. In the second portion of the program, students carry out a workshop in expressive arts for human development and social change, through which they build skills in creative expression and group facilitation. They apply these skills in the design of a workshop for creative expression of community youth, and dialogue with local representatives to put it into action. Each student receives intensive guidance from experienced facilitators to complete this process.
